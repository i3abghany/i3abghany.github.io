Privacy

This is a personal academic homepage. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party content. The short version: nothing you do here is measured, and nothing about your visit reaches anyone but the company hosting the files.

Cookies

None. This site sets no cookies of any kind.

Local storage

One item is stored in your browser's local storage: an entry named theme, whose value is either dark or light. It is written only when you press the light/dark toggle, and it exists so the site remembers that choice on your next visit.

It never leaves your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is not used to recognise, count, or profile you. You can remove it at any time by clearing site data for this domain in your browser settings.

Third parties

There are none while the page loads. Fonts and icons are served from this domain rather than from Google Fonts or a CDN, so no outside company is told your IP address merely because you opened this page.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and request details in its server logs in order to deliver the page and to protect the service against abuse. This is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

GitHub Pages provides me with no visitor statistics or logs, so I neither receive nor can access that information.

Links out

Links to other sites - publishers, Google Scholar, LinkedIn, GitHub - are ordinary links, and those sites apply their own privacy policies once you follow one. Nothing is loaded from them beforehand, and the links are marked rel="noreferrer" so the destination is not told which page you came from.

Email

If you write to me, your message sits in my university mailbox and is used only to reply. It is not added to any list.

Contact

Questions about this page, or about any data relating to you, can go to mahmoud_abumandour@sfu.ca. If you are in the EU, EEA, or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.
